Final-year project

EV traction motor modelling

My project examines how an EV traction motor’s efficiency and losses vary with the way the vehicle is driven. I use drive cycles to relate changes in vehicle speed to the motor’s operating points.

Drive-Cycle-Based Efficiency Mapping and Loss Modelling of an EV Traction Motor

Choose a section to see the research question, the planned workflow or the intended outputs.

Research question

Where does an EV traction motor operate over a drive cycle, and how do motor efficiency and losses vary across those operating points?

Modelling steps

  1. Start with a drive cycle. Use a vehicle speed profile over time.
  2. Estimate motor operating points. Use a vehicle model to relate the drive cycle to motor speed and torque.
  3. Assess efficiency and losses. Relate the operating points to the efficiency map and loss model.

Planned outputs

The planned outputs are a speed–torque operating-point plot, a motor efficiency map and an analysis of motor losses over the selected cycles.
