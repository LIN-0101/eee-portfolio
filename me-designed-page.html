Final-year project

Drive-Cycle-Based Efficiency Mapping and Loss Modelling of an EV Traction Motor

The project links changes in vehicle speed over a drive cycle to the motor’s operating conditions. It examines where the motor operates on an efficiency map and how motor losses vary across those conditions.

Read the modelling approach

The planned workflow starts with vehicle speed profiles from selected drive cycles. These profiles will be used to estimate motor speed and torque over time. The resulting operating points will support efficiency mapping and loss modelling.
